CLARITY LANDING PAGE
====================
Responsive Bootstrap 5 landing page.

Technology Stack behavior:
- Click Frontend to show its 6 technology cards.
- Click Backend, Mobile, Cloud & DevOps, or Database to switch to that category's 6 cards.
- Only the selected category is displayed, matching the supplied design reference.

Run: Extract the ZIP, open the folder in VS Code, then open index.html with Live Server.
